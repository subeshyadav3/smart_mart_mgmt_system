# Smart Mart Management System

## Project Overview
Smart Mart Management System is a software engineering project designed to automate and manage daily operations of a retail mart. The system includes employee authentication, inventory management, billing, customer membership, and reporting features.

---

## Main Objectives
- Manage products and inventory efficiently
- Provide secure authentication for workers and admins
- Generate bills and sales records
- Manage customer memberships and loyalty points
- Monitor employee activities and sales reports

---

## Main Modules

### 1. Authentication & Role Management
- Secure login/logout
- Role-based access control
- Admin, Manager, Cashier roles

### 2. Product & Inventory Management
- Add, update, and remove products
- Manage stock quantities
- Low stock alerts
- Product categories

### 3. Billing & Sales Module
- Generate customer bills
- Calculate totals and discounts
- Payment handling
- Automatic inventory update after sales

### 4. Membership & Customer Management
- Customer registration
- Membership ID generation
- Loyalty points system
- Purchase history tracking

### 5. Employee Management
- Employee profiles
- Attendance management
- Activity tracking

---

## Technologies Used
- Frontend: Next.js + TypeScript
- Backend: Node.js + Express.js
- Database: PostgreSQL
- ORM: Prisma
- Authentication: JWT + bcrypt
- Styling: Tailwind CSS
